Nature's Alternative to
A Plastic Planet

Welcome to LeafCycle. We are dedicated to replacing everyday non-renewable items with renewable natural resources. Scroll down to see how we are changing the world, one leaf at a time.

The Problem We Solve

Everyday items like plastic baggies and standard paper towels rely on non-renewable resources that devastate our environment. Plastics can burn or persist in landfills for over a century.

Environmental Project Metrics

Our goals and limits defined mathematically for our initiative.

1. Plastic Lifespan (yrs): p > 100
2. Volunteer Hours: h ≥ 40
3. Scholarship Value ($): s ≥ 1000
4. Carbon Saved (lbs): c ≥ 500
5. Leaves Used: L > 10000
6. Waste Produced (kg): w ≤ 0.5
7. Camp Attendees: a ≥ 20
8. Deforestation Rate: d ≤ 0
9. Compost Time (mo): t < 3
10. Baggies Replaced: b ≥ 150
11. Water Saved (gal): W > 200
12. Energy Used (kWh): e < 50
13. Student Age: A ≥ 14
14. Toxicity Level: T ≤ 0
15. Renewability (%): R ≥ 99

How We Do It

1. Source

Gathering fallen leaves and natural sources.

2. Process

Pressing and binding natural fibers.

3. Replace

Replacing household plastics with earth-safe goods.
